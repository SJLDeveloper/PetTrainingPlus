# Changelog

## 1.8.1 (2026-11-23): first public release

WoW Pet Training+ replaces the hunter Beast Training window in WoW: Forever.

- **Every pet skill on one line, all ranks grouped.** Each rank shows whether you know it, whether your current pet has
  it, whether it can be trained right now, whether you could get it at your level, or whether you haven't learned it.
  A rank your pet has but you don't (right after a tame) is striped.
- **Only what your pet can learn**, with one large toggle to show everything again.
- **Beast Training, framed together.** Pet Training+ opens beside Blizzard's own window inside one border. Pick a rank
  in either list and press Train; after training, the next rank is ready. Esc or either X closes both.
- **Learn Log.** Every rank you learn is logged with the creature your pet was tamed from, where, and when; every tame
  is logged with what the beast knew.
- **Forever Bestiary link.** Right-click any skill to open it in Forever Bestiary (Pet Hunter).
- **Loyalty bar.** Your pet's loyalty level and a bar that fills towards the next level with a countdown, turning gold
  ("Loyalty Increase Imminent!") in the last seconds, pausing while you rest in a city or inn, and disappearing at Best
  Friend.

### Known limitations
- **Loyalty estimates are approximate.** The game hides loyalty points, so the timings come from measured play: from
  level 2 on, each level takes about 15 minutes more than the last at a normal rate (30, 45, 60, 75 minutes), which
  has matched closely on two pets. The first level after a tame varies (about 12 to 15 minutes so far), so its estimate
  can be a few minutes off. Pet Training+ records your own levels and uses them for your estimates once it has them.
- Blizzard's Beast Training list shows about 7 rows and addons can't scroll it: if the rank you picked is further down,
  scroll Blizzard's list until it lights up.
- Forever's Beast Training lists only what your current pet's family can learn: open it once with each family you use.
